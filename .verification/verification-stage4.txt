Features:
  real terminal caret: render.Buffer carries the frame's caret, Canvas.SetCaret
    maps local coordinates through the active clip, and the VT adapter emits CUP
    plus DECTCEM transitions after the cell runs that move the cursor
  left-button drag selection in ui.TextInput, including one-grapheme autoscroll
    past either edge, with no undo history recorded for a selection
  automatic wheel bubbling from an exhausted scrollable region to the region
    that geometrically encloses it, with ScrollStyle.Passive for containers
  Button and List draw in their own coordinates and rely on canvas clipping, so
    a half-scrolled control stays aligned with its content
  showcase: the controls panel is a passive viewport holding the nested
    million-row log and the frame telemetry, so nothing is dropped when the
    terminal is short
  VT reference-grid conformance test for the encoder
  one-pass horizontal window in the editor, replacing a quadratic search
