Mechanism (pinned by tests, traced to code):
  - authority: every cell decision measures one grapheme via
    cellWidthOf -> terminalWidthOf(grapheme, policy); the canvas
    variant uses the canvas's own policy, so wrap decisions and drawn
    cells cannot disagree (TestWrapPolicyChangesBreakes,
    TestCanvasTextWrapFollowsCanvasPolicy)
  - EGC safety: graphemes are appended whole to builders; splitRun
    re-iterates a run only when it is wider than the limit; a wide
    grapheme that would straddle the limit wraps early and leaves the
    last cell empty (TestCanvasTextWrapDrawsWideGapAndNeverSplits:
    spacer cell "" at x+1 per buffer convention)
  - unprintable truth (the fuzz fix): control graphemes and invalid
    bytes are kept in the output at zero cells, mirroring
    Canvas.Text's draw skip. Dropping them (the first
    implementation) let neighbors merge across the deletion: input
    "man ZWJ 0xA9 girl 0" wrapped into a line "manZWJgirl0" whose
    re-segmentation joined man+ZWJ+girl into ONE cluster, so the line
    measured as drawn by nobody. Keeping the byte preserves the
    input's cluster boundaries exactly; the corpus seed
    8207cdfcf98b0d06 fails without the fix and passes with it
  - wrap semantics: hard breaks (\n, \r\n, lone \r) always split;
    trailing hard newline yields a final empty line; soft-break
    whitespace is consumed; leading indentation is content that
    overflows as its own line when wider than the limit; NBSP is word
    content; only " " and "\t" break words; char mode keeps
    whitespace as cells; tabs keep four-column stops relative to the
    wrapped line's start (both modes; a tab wider than the limit
    overflows alone, pinned)
  - limit <= 0: hard lines only, no soft wrapping
  - TextWrap: wraps to rect.W, draws rows i < rect.H, returns FULL
    metrics, so Height > rect.H reports clipped overflow
    (TestCanvasTextWrapClipsRowsButCountsOverflow)
  - hyphenation: OFF by design and documented in the WrapOptions
    comment — a soft hyphen is a zero-width codepoint terminals
    render or drop differently, and breaking on it would measure one
    line with two width authorities

Known limits (honest, not fixed this slice):
  - a one-cell limit under wide content overflows by the wide width:
    a fresh line accepts any single grapheme rather than dropping it
    (pinned, documented in the WrapLines comment)
  - render.TextWidthWith counts a lone invalid byte as one cell
    (uniseg's replacement-char width) while Canvas.Text skips it in
    drawing; wrap deliberately follows the DRAW side (cellWidthOf
    returns 0), so MeasureWrap and the drawn cells agree even where
    TextWidthWith would overcount. The TextWidthWith quirk is
    pre-existing in types.go and untouched here
  - the wrap is stateless: TextArea-level caching of a document's
    layout is deferred to slice 5 by design

    gofmt ran instead)
